FinVerse Agent

IEEE CSCloud 2025 · second author of four
Research

A financial AI agent built for the FinAI 2025 contest. Fifteen task-specific LoRA adapters sit on a single 4-bit quantized LLaMA-3.1-8B. A Planner picks the adapter, an Executor runs it, and a Verifier checks the answer and re-routes it if the format or the numbers look wrong.

93.07% CFA Level I (GPT-4o: 88.10%) · 97.24 F1 on NER (BloombergGPT: 60.82) · 99.50% Financial Math · 1st place, FinAI 2025 Task 2
