FinVerse Agent

IEEE CSCloud 2025 · second author of four
Research

Fifteen task-specific LoRA adapters on a single 4-bit quantized LLaMA-3.1-8B, with a Planner, Executor and Verifier routing loop.

  • Beats GPT-4o on CFA exams and BloombergGPT on NER
