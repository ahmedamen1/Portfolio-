Movie Recommender from Scratch

Matrix factorization with no recommender library in it. I derived the update equations from the log joint, wrote them in NumPy, compiled the inner loops with Numba, and trained on all 32 million MovieLens ratings. Coursework at AIMS, written up in the ICML template. Sole author.

Compiling beat parallelizing

One user update over 64,301 ratings took 0.81 s in plain NumPy. Each user solves independently, so I expected this to scale with threads. It did not — the per-user work is a 10×10 solve, far too small to pay back the scheduling overhead. Compilation had already removed the real bottleneck, which was Python loop dispatch.

Two cold-start tests disagreed

A film with no ratings has no signal, so its vector is pulled toward a mean predicted from its genres. I tested that two ways and got opposite answers.

Same prior, opposite result. Lower RMSE is better.

Naturally-unrated films are not a random sample. They are obscure, and genre is a weaker guide for an obscure film than for a popular one that merely happened to be held out. My controlled experiment had quietly assumed the thing it was meant to test. I reported both, and said in the paper that the observational result is the one that reflects deployment.

What deriving it taught me

The hyperparameters are ratiosWriting the normal equations by hand made clear that the effective penalties are τ/λ and τb/λ. Changing λ alone silently moves both — easy to miss when a library exposes them as three independent knobs.
Capacity was not the bottleneckK=20 moved test RMSE by 0.001 for 2.2× the training time, while train RMSE fell much further. The extra capacity went into memorising. I kept K=10.
Precision by roleRatings in float32 to halve the memory of the one huge array; parameters and error accumulators in float64, because summing millions of residuals in float32 loses accuracy where it counts.
A bug worth writing upThe bias run logged 8 updates, then restarted its counter and logged 20 more. I refit independently to confirm all 28, corrected the axis, and made it a labelled subsection rather than a silent re-plot.
