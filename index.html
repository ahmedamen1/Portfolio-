I build AI that is
small, fast and grounded.

Big models are easy to demo and expensive to run. I care about the version that ships: cheap enough to afford, fast enough to use, and answering from its sources rather than from model memory. Most of the systems I build are required to quote the retrieved text, and I build the evaluation that proves whether they did.
