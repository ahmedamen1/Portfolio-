What I do

Three things I am good at

All of it points the same way: making capable AI cheap enough and reliable enough to put in front of real users.

Retrieval

Production RAG

Hybrid BM25 and vector search, RRF fusion, MMR reranking, intent routing. I have built this over ~100 classical Arabic books and made it answer reliably in dialect.

Efficiency

Fine-tuning LLMs

LoRA and QLoRA on quantized models. In FinVerse Agent, 15 adapters over one 4-bit LLaMA-3.1-8B beat GPT-4o on CFA exams. Small models, trained well, win more often than people expect.

Arabic NLP

Language that fits

Egyptian dialect and MSA, RTL formatting, voice in and out. Arabic is my first language, so I can tell when a model is merely fluent and when it is actually right.
