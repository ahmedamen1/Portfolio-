The Spine on WhatsApp

The same assistant reachable from three messaging platforms, behaving like a well-briefed salesperson in whichever language the customer writes. I owned the retrieval and image layer, the multilingual output, and the rate limits that keep a business account from being flagged.

What it does

From the customer's side it should feel like messaging a person who knows the inventory.

Answers in the language they wrote inJudged per message, so a customer switching mid-conversation is followed rather than corrected.
Sends the right pictures"Show me B1.1" returns that building's exterior render followed by its floor plans. A question about a 106 m² apartment returns the actual 106 m² unit plans.
Reports real inventory"What units are bigger than 70 m?" returns all 176 matching configurations across 19 buildings, grouped by bedroom count — not three illustrative examples.
Answers voice notes with voice notesSynthesised in the same dialect the customer used.
Declines cleanlyWill not invent a building that does not exist, will not discuss a competitor, and does not attach photographs to a pricing question.
Runs AI workers off-boxKafka with an external listener, so inference does not have to share a host with webhook ingestion.

See it work

The same question asked on WhatsApp and on Instagram gets two genuinely different answers, because the platforms cap replies at 4,096 and 1,000 characters. That is the same data rendered at three verbosities, not one answer truncated.

Several of the corner units add terraces of 9 to 18 m² on top of the built area. Which size range should I go into?3,172 characters on WhatsApp, where the cap is 4,096. The full answer with every size and terrace area. On Messenger this renders as ranges per bedroom type at 1,390 characters; on Instagram, totals and an offer to drill down at 518.
