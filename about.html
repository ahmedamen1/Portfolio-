About

Hi, I'm Amin

I am an AI Engineer at Takhail in Giza, where I build Arabic RAG pipelines and LLM voice agents, and lead a small team of two engineers.

My interest is resource efficiency. A model that needs a cluster to answer one question is a demo, not a product. The work I am proudest of is the opposite: fifteen adapters on one quantized 8B model that beat GPT-4o on finance exams, and offline Arabic speech recognition running on a Raspberry Pi in a wheelchair.

In September 2026 I start an MSc in AI for Science at AIMS South Africa, fully funded by a Google DeepMind scholarship.

How I work

What I check first

When a RAG answer is wrong, the model is usually the last thing at fault. I work down this list in order.

1 · intent2 · query transform 3 · retrieval4 · BM25 matches 5 · vector matches6 · reranking 7 · context passed8 · how the LLM used it 9 · post-processing 10 · only then, the prompt

The same instinct applies to existing code: read it, understand the architecture, make the smallest change that works, then test. I do not rewrite a project because I did not understand it.
